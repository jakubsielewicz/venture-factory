## Procedure
1. Pick the **value metric** — what scales with customer value (seats, usage, outcomes). It should align cost-to-serve with willingness-to-pay.
2. Design 2–3 tiers (e.g. Starter / Pro / Scale): for each, the target buyer, the 1–2 features that justify the jump, and the price point.
3. Anchor price to value delivered and to competitor pricing (cite competitor pages with dates); avoid cost-plus.
4. Add the expansion path (what grows ARPU over time) and a free-trial / freemium decision with its rationale.
5. Output a pricing table + the value-metric rationale; feed price points to `unit-economics`.

Cite competitor prices with dates. Heuristics: `knowledge/ledger.md`.
