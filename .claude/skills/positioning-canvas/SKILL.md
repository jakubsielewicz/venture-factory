## Procedure
1. Fill the canvas: target customer (from the ICP), the problem, the category / frame of reference, the competitive alternative, and the unique differentiated value.
2. Write the positioning one-liner: "For [customer] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [key benefit], unlike [alternative]."
3. Pull 3 proof points from the product's real capabilities and the `financials/` value (don't overclaim).
4. List the messages NOT to send (the compliance/safe-harbour claims `advisory/` flagged as off-limits).
5. Write to `marketing/positioning.md`.

Mirror what the product actually does. Heuristics: `knowledge/ledger.md`.
