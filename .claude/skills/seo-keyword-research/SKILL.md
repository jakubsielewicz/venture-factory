## Procedure
1. Start from the ICP's jobs-to-be-done; brainstorm seed terms, then expand to long-tail and question queries.
2. For each cluster: estimate intent (informational/commercial), rough volume, and difficulty; cite the evidence you can find.
3. Prioritise: high-intent + winnable-difficulty first (a small site can't rank for head terms on day one).
4. Map clusters to content angles (a page/post that genuinely helps and converts).
5. Write to `marketing/seo.md` with a prioritised keyword→content table.

Numbers here are directional — mark estimates as estimates. Heuristics: `knowledge/ledger.md`.
