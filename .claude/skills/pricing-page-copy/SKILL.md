## Procedure
1. Mirror the pricing model (tiers, value metric, prices) from `financials/` exactly — no invented prices.
2. For each tier: who it's for, the value in their words, the 2–3 features that justify it, and a clear CTA.
3. Write the funnel copy: headline (the positioning one-liner), the problem, the proof, objection handling, and the CTA.
4. Stay honest: claim only what the product does; include the disclaimers `advisory/` requires; never imply a regulated/safe-harbour outcome that's off-limits.
5. Write to `marketing/pricing-page.md` (draft — publishing needs human approval).

Heuristics: `knowledge/ledger.md`.
