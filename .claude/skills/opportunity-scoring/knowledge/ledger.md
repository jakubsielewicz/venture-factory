If >3 funded startups are directly targeting the same compliance obligation, cap the open-lane sub-score at 3/5 even if incumbents appear absent. - evidence: aml-tranche2 (crowded by multiple VC-backed entrants), payday-super (same mandate, multiple entrants), asleep-incumbent (single-mandate, limited startup crowding — contrasting case) - confidence: med (n=3)
