# Opportunity scoring rubric — dimension anchors

Score each dimension 1–5 using these anchors. Multiply by the weight, sum, ×4 for a 0–100 score.

## Demand & search momentum (w=0.25)
- 5: clear, growing demand — multiple dated signals (rising search, active communities, paid alternatives selling).
- 3: steady but flat demand; people look for this but the market isn't growing.
- 1: no evidence anyone is searching/paying; you'd be creating the category.

## Monetisation clarity / willingness-to-pay (w=0.20)
- 5: buyers already pay for adjacent tools; budget and buyer are obvious.
- 3: plausible willingness-to-pay but unproven; consumer-ish or discretionary.
- 1: users expect free; no clear budget holder.

## Passive-fit: low ongoing ops after build (w=0.20)
- 5: self-serve, automatable, minimal support/content treadmill after launch.
- 3: some recurring ops (support, content refresh, integrations to maintain).
- 1: high-touch — sales, manual fulfilment, constant moderation/support.

## Build feasibility for a solo/small team (w=0.15)
- 5: shippable MVP in weeks with standard tools; no exotic tech/data.
- 3: a few hard parts (an integration, a data source, some ML).
- 1: needs a team, rare expertise, or large upfront data/capital.

## Defensibility / moat potential (w=0.10)
- 5: a credible durable advantage (proprietary data, network effect, switching cost).
- 3: a head-start or brand edge but copyable.
- 1: a feature, not a business — trivially cloned.

## Regulatory drag — INVERSE (w=0.10)
- 5: no licensing/compliance burden.
- 3: manageable obligations (privacy, tax, ToS).
- 1: licensed/regulated domain (health, finance, legal) or heavy compliance.

## Notes
- Any disqualifier (hard legal block, single-platform dependency, zero willingness-to-pay) → KILL regardless of the numeric score; record why.
- Prefer a range + source over a confident point estimate. An unsourced 5 is really a 3 with optimism.
