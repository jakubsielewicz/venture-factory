## Procedure
1. Define the ICP: firmographics (size, sector, geo), the buyer + the user, the trigger that makes them buy, and where they already look for solutions.
2. Willingness-to-pay: what they pay for adjacent tools today, the budget owner, and price sensitivity (cite sources).
3. Anti-ICP: who to explicitly NOT target (saves wasted CAC).
4. At G1, keep it to a one-pager that feeds `opportunity-scoring` + `unit-economics`. At G3, expand with channels.
5. Write to `marketing/icp.md`.

Heuristics: `knowledge/ledger.md`.
