# Venture brief - revenue-vs-compliance

> Living source of truth. The venture-orchestrator is the sole writer.

## One-liner
Experiment result + lead: an AI invoice-audit-as-a-service for AU SMEs (freight/utility/telecom/card bills), paid as a % of recovered overcharges - the strongest candidate from a revenue-buyer vs compliance head-to-head.

## Current state
- **Gate:** G0 experiment COMPLETE (green). Winner B (invoice-audit) PARK 66 (conditional PURSUE on lane probe). **Thesis PARTIALLY HELD.**
- **Status:** in-progress
- **Last updated:** 2026-06-19

## The experiment
Tested: do revenue/savings tools for moneyed buyers beat penalty-avoidance compliance tools for stressed buyers? Scored 4 head-to-head under the v2 discipline:
1. B Invoice-audit (SAVE) - 66 PARK
2. A Tender win-rate (MAKE) - 60 PARK (crowded)
3. C Succession / sale-readiness (MAKE) - 55 PARK (unverified WTP + drag)
4. D Privacy Act compliance (AVOID - control) - 46 PARK
**Thesis PARTIALLY HELD:** all 3 revenue plays beat the compliance control; +20 pts at the top (B vs D), driven almost entirely by WTP (SAVE buyers 5/5 vs AVOID 2/5). But NONE cleared PURSUE - the discipline (crowding, lane cap, unverified WTP) held them at 46-66.

## Winner thesis (B - invoice-audit-as-a-service)
Documented bill-error rates (up to 20% of freight invoices; 3-8% of transport budgets; AU telecom recoveries 8-40% of spend). The contingency model (20-30% of recovered) self-liquidates WTP friction. No AU-native SaaS+contingency SME entrant found; ERA Group AU (sole incumbent) targets mid/large enterprise. Savings-funded = the cleanest WTP of anything scouted this session.

## G0 verdict
PARK 66. Conditional PURSUE -> ~71 if the ERA lane probe confirms no SME incumbent. The decisive PRODUCT risk: is invoice parsing automatable (low-ops product, passive-fit 4-5) or labour-heavy (an AI-branded consultancy, passive-fit 2-3)?

## Load-bearing evidence (verify at G1)
- Bill-error rates: up to 20% freight invoices, 3-8% transport budgets (FreightWaves/ZDSCS 2025); AU telecom recoveries 8-40% (HyeTech AU 2025). [verify]
- Contingency standard 20-30% of recovered (Apexanalytix 2025) -> near-zero WTP friction.
- No AU SaaS+contingency SME entrant found (6 searches, 2026-06-21); ERA Group AU enterprise-only, no SME pricing.

## Open risks / G1 unknowns
1. ERA Group AU SME pricing/threshold - a buyer-style probe (research/lane-test.md) swings the score 60->71. Make-or-break lane.
2. Recoverable $/AU SME - AU-specific bill-error rates + average bill sizes unverified; unit economics need enough recoverable per client for a ~25% contingency to work.
3. Ops intensity per client - automatable parsing (scalable product) vs per-engagement human labour (consultancy). Determines passive-fit AND whether it's the right model at all.

## Strategic takeaway (candidate curator heuristic)
**Buyer economics is an under-weighted lever:** SAVE/MAKE buyers score far better on willingness-to-pay than penalty-AVOID buyers (5/5 vs 2/5 here). Candidate rule: prefer savings-funded / revenue-generating plays; treat penalty-avoidance compliance as carrying a structural WTP ceiling. n=1 (this experiment) - corroborate before promoting to a SKILL.md.
