# Longlist — advantage-zone-sweep G0 discovery sweep

**Date:** 2026-07-20 · **Author:** opportunity-scout (G0)

Scope note: this sweep deliberately excludes re-litigating sectors already scouted in this portfolio
(AU aged/health-care compliance — `cyber-defence-au`, `support-at-home-claims`, `app17-adm-transparency`,
plus other aged/health-care-adjacent scouts since parked or removed from the active portfolio; SME pay
compliance — `au-sme-compliance`; AML Tranche-2 real estate/precious metals — `aml-tranche2`;
electrification rebates, payday super, invoice-audit-as-a-service). All 16
candidates below are genuinely new territory: fintech/reg reporting, ESG/sustainability disclosure,
property/strata, legal ops, construction, gov procurement, franchising.

Six hard gates applied to every candidate: **(i)** launchable ≤90 days at 10-15 hrs/wk · **(ii)** ≤AUD $5k
capital to first revenue · **(iii)** evidence of EXISTING SPEND or acutely paid pain (not hypothetical) ·
**(iv)** a nameable, reachable first-10-customers channel · **(v)** defensibility beyond raw model
capability (crowding check: >3 active/funded competitors chasing the same mandate = fail) · **(vi)** a real
why-now catalyst.

## Pattern observed across this sweep
Every AU regulatory-compliance idea that looked plausible on the "law is real" test **already has an
entrenched incumbent or a swarm of boutique consultants occupying the exact lane** — StrataMax/MRI (strata),
Cm3/Avetta (contractor prequalification), Equifax (PPSR), LeaseInfo (lease abstraction), iPRO/Walk Free
(modern slavery), 11 named vendors (AASB S2/climate), Payapps (construction claims), InfoTrack-class
resellers (registry search, per `aml-tranche2` precedent). This is the same structural finding as the
portfolio's aged/health-care PARK cluster, just manifesting as **crowding** rather than **buyer financial
stress**. Only two candidates (#1 Franchise, and conditionally #2 AASB S2 despite its crowding) cleared
enough of the six gates to warrant a full deep-dive; see `research/opportunity-score.md`.
