## ICP Affordability Threshold

Target buyer: Australian SME employer, 2–20 employees, award-covered industry. Willingness-to-pay for a standalone audit tool (not a full payroll suite) estimated at AUD $49–$99/month. A competitor priced at or below AUD $99/month for a comparable workflow closes the lane.

## Competitor Pricing Direct Check

### Employment Hero

**Public pricing (fetched 2026-06-21, employmenthero.com/pricing):**
- HR Essentials: AU$10/emp/mo (min AU$100/mo)
- HR Engage: AU$14/emp/mo (min AU$140/mo)
- Employment Unlimited: custom pricing, includes intelligent payroll with award interpretation

**Award Costing feature (June 2025 product update):** Available to "all Employment Hero Payroll connected organisations" — covers Roster, Timesheet, Classification, and Shift Conditions. [Source: employmenthero.com/blog/product-update-june-2025/]

**HeroForce (2025):** Co-employment model, 3–5% of total payroll as service fee. Includes fully managed modern award interpretation. Targets SMEs. [Source: dynamicbusiness.com, 2025]

**Lane assessment vs ICP:** The minimum AU$100/mo for HR Essentials lands AT the ICP ceiling of AU$99/mo threshold. The Award Costing feature is now built in. However, this is a full HR suite — the SME paying $100/mo gets onboarding, contracts, policies, and more, not just a compliance audit. It is not a standalone payroll-data-ingestion + classification-check tool. A 2-employee cafe owner does not need or want to migrate to Employment Hero to run a once-a-year audit.

**VERDICT:** Pricing straddles the ICP threshold; Award Costing is bundled into a full-suite product that requires migration. Does NOT directly close the standalone-audit lane, but significantly reduces the gap's distinctiveness.

### Deputy

**Public pricing (fetched 2026-06-21, deputy.com/au/pricing):**
- Lite: AU$6.75/user/mo
- Core: AU$8.75/user/mo (most popular)
- Pro: AU$13/user/mo
- Payroll add-on: AU$5/user/mo (free until January 2027 promotional)
- Minimum: AU$30/mo

**Award Interpretation:** Included at no extra cost in all tiers. "Advanced Award Interpretation" in Core+. Deputy Payroll AU (launched June 2025) includes built-in award interpretation.

**Lane assessment vs ICP:** Deputy is firmly below the ICP affordability threshold (AU$30 minimum). It targets rostering/shift-work businesses. It does NOT offer:
- A standalone "upload your CSV and get an audit report" workflow
- A safe-harbour-ready PDF for Fair Work Ombudsman purposes
- Coverage for non-shift businesses (professional services, construction)

**VERDICT:** Below affordability threshold but is a roster/scheduling product, not a compliance-audit product. The lane for "platform-agnostic payroll CSV audit + safe-harbour report" remains open for non-scheduling businesses. However, Deputy is actively expanding into payroll (June 2025) and could extend its audit reporting.

### Workstem AU

**Public pricing (fetched 2026-06-21, workstem.com/au/pricing):**
- Attendance Plus: AU$7/emp/mo (annual billing; min 5 employees = AU$35/mo)
- Payroll Pro: AU$16/emp/mo (annual billing; min 5 employees = AU$80/mo)
- Covers 122+ modern awards and 34 EBAs

**Lane assessment vs ICP:** Payroll Pro at AU$80/mo for 5 employees is BELOW the ICP's upper affordability threshold (AU$99/mo) and substantially below the original G0 wedge claim. This is a full self-serve payroll system — a SME that adopts Workstem gets award-interpreted payroll processing, not just a once-yearly audit. But switching payroll systems is a high-friction decision for an SME already on Xero/MYOB.

**VERDICT:** Workstem pricing closes the "price-band gap" for SMEs WILLING to switch payroll systems. The standalone audit-only lane (no payroll system migration required) is still unoccupied. However, this is a stronger competitive threat than recognised in the original teardown.

### WageSafe

**Public pricing:** UNKNOWN — no pricing page; demo-only. [Source: wagesafe.com.au, fetched 2026-06-21] Founded 2021; no funding raised; 14 employees. Enterprise-positioned (continuous monitoring, integrates with HRIS).

**Lane assessment:** Enterprise or mid-market pricing likely above SME ICP threshold, but unverified. Probe required (see email template below).

### PaidRight / Wrkr

**Public pricing:** UNKNOWN — not disclosed. Acquired December 2025 / February 2026 for A$11.4M by Wrkr Ltd (ASX: WRK). [Source: paidright.io acquisition announcement; newsnreleases.com, December 2025] Targets 100+ employee organisations; enterprise pricing.

**Lane assessment:** Clearly out of SME price range. Not a lane threat to the $49–$99/mo ICP.

## Platform Risk Assessment

**Employment Hero (KeyPay engine):** Now natively building award-rate compliance checking. Award Costing launched June 2025. HeroForce adds AI-driven compliance monitoring. Employment Hero is moving TOWARD the exact wedge this product occupies — this is the same platform risk that PARKed the payday-super venture. [Source: employmenthero.com/blog/product-update-june-2025/]

**Deputy:** Launched payroll with award interpretation June 2025. Active expansion into compliance reporting. Not yet an audit-PDF product, but the trajectory is in that direction.

**Xero/MYOB:** Xero does NOT natively interpret awards — confirmed as of 2025. [Source: productideas.xero.com/forums — open idea thread for award wages; xero.com/au/guides/modern-awards-hybrid-workforce-payroll/] MYOB shows limits with complex awards. These two platforms remain partial complements rather than direct threats for the CSV-audit workflow.

**Net platform risk verdict:** ELEVATED. Employment Hero and Deputy are both building natively into the compliance-audit space. The standalone-CSV-audit wedge is a 12–24 month window, not a durable structural moat. This is material new information since the original G0 scoring.
