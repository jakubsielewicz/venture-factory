## SECTION 3 — HOW IT WORKS

### Header

> Three steps to an audit-ready compliance report

**Step 1 — Upload your payroll CSV**
Export from Xero, MYOB, Reckon, or any spreadsheet. No special template required. Our column-mapping screen handles real-world CSV formats automatically.

**Step 2 — We map your staff to the right award and check their rates**
Answer a short questionnaire about your industry and primary award. Our classification engine maps each employee to the correct award level and compares their current pay rate against the current Fair Work Commission rate for that classification. Each employee gets a confidence score (HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW) on their classification accuracy.

**Step 3 — Download your audit-ready PDF report**
Your report shows: every employee checked, their award classification, the current FWC rate, any pay gap (in dollars per hour and per week), and the date of the check. It includes the solicitor-approved disclaimer language required to correctly represent what a compliance check does and does not establish.

> **Note:** LOW-confidence classifications should be verified with the FWO Find My Award tool or your employment adviser before you rely on those results. We flag these clearly before you download.

## SECTION 5 — OBJECTION HANDLING

### "I already use Xero/MYOB — doesn't it handle award compliance?"

Xero and MYOB manage payroll calculations based on the pay rate you enter. They do not independently verify that the rate you entered is correct for the employee's award level and classification. This tool checks that the rate you're paying matches the rate you're required to pay.

### "Can't I just use the Fair Work Pay and Conditions Tool for free?"

Yes, for individual lookups — if you already know the correct award and classification for each employee. The FWO tool produces no audit trail and requires you to search one employee at a time. If you have 10 employees across two awards, that's 10 manual searches and no downloadable record. This tool handles a full payroll CSV in one upload and produces a dated, downloadable report.

### "Isn't this something my accountant should be doing?"

Your accountant can help with award interpretation. So can we — for less than 30 minutes of their time per month, with a structured output they can attach to your file. Many bookkeepers and accountants use this tool to run a check before their clients' financial year-end review.

### "What if the tool gets a classification wrong?"

Every employee in your report gets a confidence score. HIGH means we're confident in the classification. MEDIUM means you should review it. LOW means you need to verify the award classification with the FWO Find My Award tool or your employment adviser before relying on the result. We flag LOW-confidence employees prominently before you download. The disclaimer on every report makes clear that this tool does not substitute for professional advice on individual cases.
