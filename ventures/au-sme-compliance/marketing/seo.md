## 4. Quick Wins (low difficulty, winnable within 6 months for a new domain)

A new domain cannot rank for high-difficulty head terms (e.g., "minimum wage australia") against fairwork.gov.au and major news outlets. The strategy is to own the long-tail, high-intent, low-competition terms first.

1. **Award-specific hub pages** — the combination of "award name + pay rates + year" is a long-tail cluster with low difficulty and commercially intent-ready visitors. A new domain with high-quality, sourced content can rank in 3–6 months.
2. **"Am I paying my staff correctly Australia"** — a question-format query with low difficulty; a well-structured FAQ page or blog post ranks quickly.
3. **FWC annual review seasonal content** — publishing before the June–July peak and targeting "award rate increase july 2026" captures seasonal volume with low time-to-rank.

## 5. SEO Technical Requirements (mandatory before publishing)

- SSR on landing page and all content pages (Next.js SSR is confirmed in c4.md — Vercel AU region); ensures Googlebot crawls rendered content
- Canonical URLs on all award hub pages (avoid content duplication across rate-table variations)
- Schema markup: FAQ schema on award hub pages; Article schema on blog posts; Product schema on pricing page
- Mobile performance: Lighthouse Performance >= 85 on mobile (prd.md US-01 acceptance criterion)
- Page meta title and description for all P1 pages should be written before launch — not auto-generated
- All content citing legislation must include date ("as at [date]") to avoid stale ranking with outdated information
