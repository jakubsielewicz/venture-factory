## Verdict: FAIL

The regulatory backdrop is real and dated (special payments, ACQSC refund powers, Oct 2026 billing change — see the four load-bearing claims verified in `research/opportunity-score.md`), and named large operators are visibly in pain over claiming friction and pricing exposure. But every piece of *willingness-to-pay* evidence found belongs to a different buyer tier (the largest home-care providers, already served by Ansell Strategic and Mirus Australia) than the 50–500-participant segment this idea targets, and two funded incumbents already running in that exact mid-size segment (ShiftCare, AlayaCare) are absorbing claims-quality into the core software rather than leaving it for a downstream report. This is a FAIL under the standard ≥60 PASS / 35–59 WEAK / <35 FAIL bands, and under the standard G0 procedure would stop the pipeline here (PARK on signal grounds). Per the orchestrator's explicit instruction for this pressure-test session, `research/opportunity-score.md` was produced anyway to deliver the requested six-hard-gate checklist and the comparison to the operator's 81/90 self-score — treat that downstream scoring as diagnostic, not as license to proceed past this conviction gate at G1.
