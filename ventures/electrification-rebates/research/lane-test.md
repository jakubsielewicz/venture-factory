## Competitor Price Probe 1: Alitsy

**Subject:** Pricing enquiry — cross-scheme certificate management for small solar+battery installer

Hi Alitsy team,

I run a small solar and battery installation business based in [VIC/NSW]. We're currently doing around 15-20 installs per month — a mix of solar PV, batteries (we're now doing CHBP claims), and heat pumps (we do VEU jobs in Victoria).

At the moment we're using a mix of spreadsheets, Greenbank for our STCs, and manually logging into the VEU Registry separately. It's getting unwieldy, especially now that CHBP battery STC requirements have changed and we need to track tiered calculations and individual module serial numbers.

2. Do you cover all three of our schemes in a single workflow: federal STC/CHBP battery STC, Victorian VEECs (heat pump activity 1D/3C and activity 6), and the NSW PDRS (we do occasional jobs cross-border)?
3. Can installers self-lodge claims directly through the platform, or does it prepare the paperwork which we then lodge ourselves?
4. Is there a trial period available?

Thanks for your help.

## Competitor Price Probe 2: Ecovantage

**To:** sales team via ecovantage.com.au contact form
**Subject:** Installer services enquiry — multi-scheme management, small solar+battery business

Hi Ecovantage,

I operate a solar, battery and heat pump installation business (~15-20 jobs/month). We're across STCs, CHBP battery, VEECs (VIC), and PDRS (NSW).

At the moment we work with separate agents for STCs and VEECs, which means two sets of paperwork, two payment schedules, and no single view of our rebate pipeline.

I'd like to understand how Ecovantage works for small installers:

1. Do you charge a flat subscription/platform fee, or is your fee built into the certificate price spread?

3. Do I retain direct access to my job records and audit trail, or does Ecovantage hold the data?
4. How do you handle the new CHBP tiered STC calculation from May 2026? Do I need to do anything differently?
5. Are there any minimum volume requirements?

I'm also considering building my own capability to self-create STCs through the REC Registry rather than assigning to an agent. What would be the key considerations in making that call?

Thanks,
