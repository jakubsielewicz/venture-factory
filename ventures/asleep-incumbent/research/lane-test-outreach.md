# Lane-test outreach — incumbent SMS pricing enquiry

**Purpose:** resolve G1 GO-IF gate #1 (the binary lane test) — does any incumbent offer an
**affordable self-serve SMS for a small (1–20 truck) operator**? Send the email below to
ATCC, Logmaster, and Kynection as a genuine prospective buyer (a 2-truck operator preparing
for the HVNL 2026 SMS obligation). Fill the placeholders; send from a real inbox.

**Decision rule (record the answers in this file):**
- Any vendor offers a **self-serve / online-signup SMS at ≤ A$130/mo for ~2 trucks, software-only** → **lane CLOSING → re-score the venture toward PARK.**
- All three are **sales-led, telematics-tied, or enterprise/quote-only with no small-fleet self-serve tier** → **lane OPEN → the thesis holds.**

---

## Email template

**Subject:** HVNL 2026 SMS — pricing for a small fleet (2 trucks)

Hi [Vendor] team,

I run a small heavy-vehicle operation ([2 trucks, based in [state]]) and I'm getting ready
for the Safety Management System requirement under the HVNL changes commencing 1 August 2026.
I'm comparing a few SMS options and would appreciate some quick details:

1. Do you offer a **self-serve / online sign-up** option for a small operator like mine, or is it sales-led only?
2. What's the **price for a 2-truck operator** (and is there a 1–5 truck tier)? Monthly and annual, please.
3. Is any **telematics/hardware required**, or is it software-only?
4. Does it produce an **audit-ready, "operating" SMS** (evidence trail — pre-starts, incidents, fatigue, training records), not just document templates?
5. **Setup/onboarding fees, minimum contract term**, and is there a **free trial**?

A link to your pricing or a short written quote would be perfect.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Phone] · [Email]

## Tips for a fast, useful answer
- Send to sales/info addresses **and** use any website "get a quote / book a demo" form — quote forms often surface pricing fastest.
- Keep it short; the 5 numbered questions are doing the work. Push politely for a **number or a price-page link** if they only offer a demo.
- Log each reply here (price, self-serve y/n, hardware y/n, contract) so the lane verdict is evidence-based, not impression-based.

## Lane assessment after Logmaster (1 of 3 replies — 2026-06-19)
- The scout's premise that incumbents are "enterprise/quote-only + telematics-tied" is **already wrong for Logmaster**: software-only, transparent (~$28+GST/driver/mo full compliance suite), no setup fee, 30-day guarantee — well under the A$130/mo lane-test threshold. The G0 competitor-teardown mischaracterised it ("no self-serve SMS pricing").
- **But** Logmaster is an EWD/fatigue-**compliance suite** that "covers *many* of the items required for an SMS" — it is not sold as a purpose-built, complete, audit-aligned **2026 SMS** product. The remaining wedge survives only if (a) operators need a complete SMS beyond Logmaster's coverage, **and** (b) Logmaster/ATCC/Kynection don't simply reposition their existing suite as "your SMS" (a quarter's work).
- **Net: GO-IF gate #1 is trending toward FAIL.** This is a thin, contestable wedge and it materially weakens the open-lane premise behind the G0 74 score. Hold for ATCC + Kynection; if they're similarly cheap/software-only/SMS-covering, the lane is effectively closed → re-score to PARK.
