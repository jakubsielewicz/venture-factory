## Disqualifier Summary

No automatic disqualifiers are triggered by this risk register. The following risks are elevated to DISQUALIFIER CANDIDATE status for human review at G2:

**R1 (exposure 15) — Incumbent self-serve entry:** If ATCC or Kynection prices a self-serve SMS tier at ≤$130/month before or within 60 days of launch, the lane closes and this venture should be re-scored as PARK. This is the single most consequential pre-G2 discovery task.

**R2 (exposure 15) — Free-default WTP collapse:** If fewer than 5 of 15 operator interviews produce purchase intent at A$79/month, the financial model's base case is invalidated. The pessimistic free-default case yields A$32K–$72K ARR on a A$50K build cost — not viable for a solo developer on reasonable payback expectations.

**R10 (latent disqualifier) — Vendor registration:** Confirmed low risk based on current NHVR published material, but must be verbally verified with NHVR before launch. If an approved-software-list scheme is introduced under the HVA framework, this would become a blocker requiring product redesign or partnership.

---

## Top 3 Decisive Risks

1. **R1 — Lane-closing competitive entry (Exposure 15):** The thesis rests entirely on the self-serve lane being open. ATCC Compliance Easy is the highest-probability lane-closer. No pricing is published; a single sales call resolves this before G2.
2. **R2 — WTP vs free NHVR PDFs (Exposure 15):** The financial model's range spans "viable solo business" to "below break-even" depending on whether operators understand and pay for the "operating" evidence gap. Interview-confirmed purchase intent is the required G2 gate condition.
3. **R3 — 44-day build window (Exposure 12):** The urgency window is structurally irreversible. Missing 1 August 2026 does not kill the venture but removes the primary demand accelerant and extends break-even by 12–18 months.
