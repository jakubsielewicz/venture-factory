## G2 Blockers

### BLOCKER 1 — "No grace period" claim is UNCITED from primary source (Claim 3)
The urgency thesis depends on operators facing immediate penalties from 1 August 2026. The commencement date is verified, but the "no grace period" qualifier — which transforms a regulatory change into an emergency — is sourced only from secondary industry sites, all of which returned 404. **Required action:** locate the exact legislative provision or NHVR enforcement statement confirming no transition period. The NTC website (ntc.gov.au) timed out; retrieve the HVNL Amendment Bill text from legislation.qld.gov.au or an NHVR FAQ that addresses the transition posture.

### BLOCKER 2 — Sub-20-truck operator count (3,200–7,200) is UNCITED (Claim 12)
This is the single load-bearing number beneath all revenue model scenarios. The total accredited operator count has a confirmed 2021-22 floor (~8,198) but the 40–60% sub-20-truck split is entirely an analyst assumption with no cited source. If the true proportion is 20–30%, the SOM halves and the base-case Year-1 ARR drops to ~A$87K–A$131K. **Required action:** obtain ATA industry data, ABS Transport and Storage business-size distribution, or a direct NHVR data enquiry (NHVR: 13 6487) before G2.

### BLOCKER 3 — ATCC Compliance Easy pricing unconfirmable from public web (Claim 14)
The go-no-go memo itself flags this as a GO-IF condition. The lane claim rests on ATCC not offering a self-serve SMS tier at ≤A$130/month. Web research confirms ATCC's demo-only model but cannot rule out a privately quoted small-operator price. **Required action:** make a direct sales call or demo request to ATCC for a 2-truck operator scenario before G2 spend is authorised. This is a binary residual risk — the memo's GO-IF 1 condition.

## Non-Blocker Issues for the Record

- **Claim 5 (CoR $10,000 penalty):** The figure is corroborated by multiple secondaries but applies narrowly to one CoR sub-duty, not all CoR breaches. General CoR primary-duty exposure is far higher (~$300K/$3M). The framing understates risk, not overstates it; direction of argument is unchanged. Suggest tightening the claim to "penalties for specific CoR duties (e.g., failing to ensure driver fitness) increase to A$10,000."
- **Claim 4 (jurisdictions):** Structurally supported from NHVR site absence of WA/NT, not explicitly confirmed by text. Low risk; standard HVNL coverage is public knowledge.
- **Claim 17 (SafetyCulture $24/seat):** Figure confirmed on official pricing page; currency strongly implied as AUD but not explicitly stated. Risk: if USD, the comparison shifts (USD $24 ≈ AUD $37). Fetch the currency footnote before using in marketing copy.
- **Claim 20 (Stripe rate):** Standard published rate, likely correct; fetch stripe.com/au/pricing to formally confirm.
- **Claim 21 (developer rate A$130/hr):** Plausible, no primary URL cited. Low risk — sensitivity to build cost is not material in the model.
