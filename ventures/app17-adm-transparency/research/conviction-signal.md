## Verdict: FAIL

The regulatory mechanism behind this venture is real, dated, and correctly characterised (see `verification-app17.md`) — this is not a fabricated "why now." But **conviction is a distinct question from whether the underlying law is real**: it asks whether *buyers* are showing recurring, intense, paying-or-willing-to-pay signal, and on that specific question the evidence found is almost entirely inferred from the statute's existence plus professional-services anticipation, not from buyer behaviour. Per the crew's standard procedure this FAIL would normally stop the pipeline before market-sizing/competitor-teardown/opportunity-scoring (report as a PARK on signal grounds).
