## Thesis
A large, imminent forcing function: AML/CTF Amendment Act 2024 (passed 29 Nov 2024); obligations for ~80-100k newly-regulated entities from 1 Jul 2026, enrolment opened 31 Mar 2026. But the law/conveyancing segment is being closed by InfoTrack's FREE compliance centre bundled into LEAP (7,000 practitioners reached, Mar 2026). The only un-owned wedge is real-estate agents (~44,900) + dealers.

## G0 verdict
**PARK - 63/100.** Real demand, but a free incumbent already took the easiest segment; the remaining RE/dealer wedge is real but smaller (SOM ~$400-500K ARR) and lives or dies on RE/dealer WTP + whether InfoTrack extends its free model.

## Open risks / G1 unknowns
1. WTP for RE agents + dealers at ~$1,200-1,800/yr (vs DIY/AUSTRAC templates) - 10-15 interviews. The thesis lives or dies here.
2. InfoTrack moat depth - will it extend the free model to RE agents (Securexchange) / accountants? If yes, the wedge closes.
3. Privacy Act (APP 11) / data-residency / SOC2 obligations for handling KYC PII at scale as a service provider.
