Generated: 2026-06-19

## Competitor landscape overview

The Tranche 2 AML compliance software market in Australia has gone from zero to crowded in under 18 months. At least 10 purpose-built products are live or near-live as of June 2026. The analysis below covers the four most significant threats, plus the DIY/spreadsheet baseline.

---

## 1. InfoTrack AML/CTF Compliance Centre

**Positioning:** "Complimentary, all-inclusive" compliance tool bundled into legal practice management systems (LEAP integration confirmed). Developed with Grant Thornton (AUSTRAC advisers). Targeted at lawyers, conveyancers, accountants, and real estate agents.

**Pricing:** No platform subscription fee. Per-search charges for VOI, PEP/sanctions, adverse media after 30 June 2026 free-trial period. (Source: InfoTrack website, fetched June 2026.)

**Gaps:**
- Deep PMS integration only via InfoTrack-partnered systems (primarily LEAP). Firms not on LEAP must connect separately.
- Per-check model: a firm with 300 verifications/year at ~$15–25/check pays $4,500–$7,500/yr in search costs — potentially more expensive than a fixed-fee subscription competitor for mid-volume firms.
- Real estate agents access it via Securexchange (separate InfoTrack product) — not a seamless single-panel experience for agents outside that ecosystem.
- Accountants using Xero/MYOB-based practice management are not natively covered.

**Switching costs:** High for LEAP users (deep integration). Low for others who have no integration to leave.

**Platform dependency:** InfoTrack/LEAP partnership. Risk: if a firm migrates away from LEAP, InfoTrack loses the channel.

**Last meaningful update:** Roadshow to 7,000 practitioners in March 2026. Active product development.

**Threat rating:** HIGH for lawyers/conveyancers on LEAP. MEDIUM for other segments. The free offer is a category-killer for the basic use case within its ecosystem.

Sources: InfoTrack compliance centre page (https://www.infotrack.com.au/aml-ctf-compliance-centre/); Lawyers Weekly March 2026 (https://www.lawyersweekly.com.au/biglaw/43062-infotrack-leads-the-profession-on-aml-ctf-readiness-a-roadshow-for-2026-and-beyond)

## 2. Arctic Intelligence — AML Accelerate

**Positioning:** Global SME-focused AML risk assessment and program platform. Active in 30+ countries, 30+ industry sectors. Partnership with CA ANZ (15% introductory discount for members). Designed specifically for "gatekeeper professions" including lawyers, accountants, real estate agents, dealers.

**Pricing:** From USD $2,500/year (~AUD $3,800/yr). CA ANZ members get 15% off Year 1. No disclosed per-check pricing — appears to be subscription-only. (Source: Arctic Intelligence product page, fetched June 2026; FinTech Australia release.)

**Gaps:**
- Price is the highest in the pure self-serve segment — ~2.5× TrustEasy and ~3.5× AML Shield starter pricing.
- US/global product with multi-country scope; may feel overly complex or generic for an AU-specific small firm.
- No deep practice management integration visible.
- The compliance program and risk assessment are strong; KYC/CDD execution features less prominent.

**Switching costs:** Moderate — compliance programs documented in platform create some data lock.

**Platform dependency:** Standalone; no single-PMS dependency.

**Last meaningful update:** CA ANZ partnership active 2025–2026. 14-day free trial launched (FinTech Australia, 2025).

**Threat rating:** MEDIUM for accountants (via CA ANZ channel). Lower for lawyers (no Law Society partnership visible) and real estate (no REIA partnership visible).

Sources: Arctic Intelligence pricing (https://arctic-intelligence.com/platforms/free-trial-aml-risk-solution-aml-accelerate); FinTech Australia (https://www.fintechaustralia.org.au/newsroom/arctic-intelligence-launches-14-day-free-trial-of-aml-accelerate); Accountants Daily: InfoTrack + CA ANZ (https://www.accountantsdaily.com.au/technology/22457-infotrack-ca-anz-team-up-to-give-members-access-to-aml-ctf-support-tools)

## 3. AML Shield

**Positioning:** Australian-built, Tranche 2-specific. Automates AML/CTF program based on AUSTRAC starter kits. Claims "unlimited CDD checks with no per-check fees" as key differentiator. Targets SMEs up to 15 employees.

**Pricing (as fetched June 2026 from amlshield.com.au/pricing/):**
- Starter Kit: $799/yr (program documents, policies, risk assessment only; no KYC/CDD tools)
- Micro: $99/mo or $1,057/yr (up to 3 employees, full suite including KYC/CDD, training)
- Standard: $149/mo or $1,591/yr (up to 15 employees, full suite + customisation)
- Pro/Reseller: contact sales

**Gaps:**
- AML program + policy focus; KYC/CDD is included but depth of identity verification (biometrics, government database checks) unclear from public materials.
- No explicit professional-body channel partnerships found.
- "Up to 3 employees" Micro tier will exclude most law/accounting practices (median 5–10 staff).
- No practice management integration evident.

**Switching costs:** Low — compliance documents are exportable PDFs; no deep workflow integration creates minimal lock-in.

**Platform dependency:** Standalone.

**Last meaningful update:** Current pricing page active June 2026 (fetched).

**Threat rating:** MEDIUM — price-competitive, AU-specific, but likely lacks depth for more complex practices and has no channel partnership advantage.

Sources: AML Shield pricing (https://www.amlshield.com.au/pricing/); AML Shield overview (https://www.amlshield.com.au/)

## 4. TrustEasy (accounting-specific)

**Positioning:** First-mover for Australian accounting firms specifically. "First platform in Australia to deliver an AML/CTF solution built exclusively for accounting firms." Integrated with trust accounting workflows. Includes AUSTRAC compliance reporting.

**Pricing (as fetched June 2026 from trusteasy.com.au):**
- AML/CTF Pro: $1,200/yr (2 users, 2,400 manual CDD, 60 online ID checks, 5 company checks)
- AML/CTF High: $1,500/yr (5 users)
- AML/CTF Ultimate: $2,400/yr (15 users)
- White Glove Onboarding add-on: $330 one-off

**Gaps:**
- Accounting-only focus means it does not serve lawyers, real estate agents, or dealers.
- Relatively low online ID check allocations at entry tier (60 checks/yr) — a busy practice could exceed this quickly.
- MYOB/Xero integration likely; LEAP/legal PMS integration irrelevant to their target.

**Switching costs:** Moderate — compliance history, CDD records, and staff training logs stored in platform create some switching friction.

**Platform dependency:** Standalone for AML; integrates with accounting practice management tools.

**Last meaningful update:** Active beta → launch for Tranche 2, Insights For Accountants coverage 2025–2026.

**Threat rating:** HIGH within accounting practices. LOW across all other professions.

Sources: TrustEasy pricing (https://trusteasy.com.au/aml-ctf-compliance-for-accounting-firms/); Insights For Accountants (https://www.insightsforaccountants.com.au/trusteasy-the-platform-accounting-firms-are-turning-to-for-aml-ctf-compliance/)

## 5. DIY / AUSTRAC Starter Kits + Spreadsheets (the manual baseline)

**Positioning:** AUSTRAC published free "Program Starter Kits" for accountants, lawyers, real estate agents, and conveyancers. These are Word/PDF templates. Firms use these with Excel/Google Sheets for CDD record-keeping.

**Price:** Free (but significant time cost — estimated 20–40 hours initial setup + ongoing manual effort).

**Gaps:** No automation, no integrated identity verification, no AUSTRAC reporting workflow, no ongoing monitoring/alerts, no training tracking. High error risk. No audit trail.

**Switching costs:** Zero — firms move to software when pain becomes acute.

**Threat rating:** MEDIUM short-term (deadline pressure forces action); LOW long-term (ongoing compliance burden makes software ROI clear).

Sources: AUSTRAC Starter Kits via Law Society NSW (https://www.lawsociety.com.au/amlctf-tranche-2-reforms-introducing-austracs-program-starter-kits); Law Society Journal (https://lsj.com.au/articles/aml-ctf-tranche-2-reforms-introducing-austracs-program-starter-kits/)

## Wedge thesis

**The wedge is real-estate agents + dealers + cross-profession multi-site firms**, with a second-order wedge in **accountants who want deeper KYB (beneficial ownership, trust structures) than TrustEasy's entry tier provides**.

InfoTrack owns law and conveyancing via LEAP. TrustEasy is first-mover for pure accountants. Arctic Intelligence is expensive and generic. No dedicated product with strong channel partnerships exists for:
- Real estate agents and agencies (REIA/state body partnerships unclaimed by any specialist)
- Dealers in precious metals/stones and trust/company service providers
- Multi-profession practices (e.g., a firm offering legal + financial + accounting services)
- Firms needing deeper beneficial ownership / trust chain mapping

The one-sentence wedge: **A Tranche 2 platform that wins via the real estate and dealer segments — underserved by both InfoTrack (no deep RE play beyond Securexchange) and TrustEasy (accounting-only) — and builds verified professional-body channel partnerships before incumbents consolidate those relationships.**

## Watch-outs
1. **Practice management bundling threat is real and accelerating.** LEAP has integrated InfoTrack; First AML has integrated with Actionstep. The pattern will continue. Any standalone product risks being displaced when the incumbent PMS vendors finalise partnerships.
2. **Compliance officer as buyer vs. principal/partner.** In most small Tranche 2 firms there is no dedicated compliance officer — the principal/partner IS the buyer. They are time-poor and will default to the cheapest credible tool, including free (InfoTrack).
3. **Pricing compression.** The presence of a free incumbency in the largest profession segment (law, ~22,000–25,000 firms) forces competitive positioning into price sensitivity or differentiation on features not in InfoTrack's scope.
