## Signal 1 — Legislative forcing function with concrete deadline
The Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Amendment Act 2024 (Cth) passed the Australian Parliament on 29 November 2024. It extends AUSTRAC obligations to approximately 80,000–100,000 newly regulated entities (up from ~17,000 currently) by 1 July 2026. Enrolment with AUSTRAC opened 31 March 2026, with a deadline of 29 July 2026 for compliance officer notification.
