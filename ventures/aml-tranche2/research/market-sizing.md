## 3. Market sizing

Self-serve or low-touch software for Tranche 2 SMEs — excludes large firms using enterprise/consulting solutions.

Roughly 85% of the 80,000 entities are SMEs (sole practitioners to ~20 staff). That gives ~68,000 SME entities.

**SAM = 68,000 × $1,500/yr ARPA = AUD $102M/yr**

### TAM (Total Addressable Market — all AU AML compliance software)
Full 80,000 entities at $1,500/yr average, plus existing 17,000 Tranche 1 entities.

**TAM = 97,000 × $1,500/yr = AUD $145M/yr**

Cross-check against top-down: IMARC Group (via OpenPR, citing IMARC research as published 2026) puts the AU AML market at USD 40.25M in 2025, growing to USD 149.84M by 2034 at 15.72% CAGR. The 2025 figure is pre-Tranche 2 (17,000 entities); the 2026 spike to ~100,000 entities is not yet reflected in the historic base. Our bottom-up SAM of ~AUD $102M (USD ~65–70M in 2026) is plausible given the ~5x entity expansion — consistent with the top-down trajectory, if above it. The top-down figures are likely understated for 2026 given the sudden entity population increase.

**SAM: AUD $85M–$120M/yr (base: $102M). TAM: AUD $130M–$170M/yr.**

Note: These figures depend on the $1,500 ARPA assumption which could be compressed by InfoTrack's free offer.

## 4. Sensitivity

The single most important input is **whether InfoTrack's free offer kills per-subscription pricing for law firms and conveyancers**. InfoTrack serves law and conveyancing via LEAP practice management integration — if it owns that channel, ~40% of the target market (lawyers + conveyancers) may default to a free tool, shrinking the addressable subscription market for a standalone product. A new entrant would need to either (a) compete on feature depth, (b) target accountants, real estate agents, and dealers who are NOT served by InfoTrack, or (c) win a different professional-body channel.

---

## 5. Verdict on size
The obtainable revenue ceiling (AUD $900K ARR at 1% SAM penetration) is worth the build for a small team, given low marginal cost of SaaS delivery. The SAM is real, non-discretionary, and multi-year (ongoing compliance obligations persist). However, the market is now competitive (8+ AU entrants already live) and one major player is free-to-platform, compressing the addressable window.

**Most sensitive input: InfoTrack's free-offer adoption rate among lawyers/conveyancers.**
